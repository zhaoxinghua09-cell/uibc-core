Every runnable vector stopped at the same gate. The corpus spells the Ed25519 JOSE algorithm "Ed25519" — in the issuer JWK, in all three WIT headers, and in the confirmation keys. Wimsey 0.9.0 accepts only the older name "EdDSA" and fails closed:

unsupported algorithm 'Ed25519', expected 'EdDSA'

Whose spelling is right?

The corpus follows the draft

draft-ietf-wimse-http-signature-08's own examples print "alg": "Ed25519" in the JWKs, and its algorithm note cites RFC 9864, which registers the fully-specified name Ed25519.

The implementation is one name behind

Wimsey v0.9.0 is pinned to draft-08, but its JWK and WIT parsers accept only EdDSA (RFC 8037) and ES256. It is self-consistent — its own tooling emits EdDSA — and it fails closed. The two sides disagree on a string, and the string is a gate.

The other tier, kept separate

Operator's own verifier (local run, 2026-11-21)

23 of the 29 vectors are single-message decidable; all 23 matched the corpus anchors, with two independent local implementations (Python, Node) returning identical verdicts and an anti-circularity injection check on the expected values. The 6 behaviour probes discriminated 6/6 against fixtures. This tier and the wimsey tier are different kinds of evidence and are not merged: the corpus matches its operator's verifier on every decidable vector, and it has not yet had a clean independent-implementation pass, because the only independent implementation available is stopped by the alg string.
