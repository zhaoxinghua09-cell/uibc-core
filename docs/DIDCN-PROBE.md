## What this supports, and what it does not

*10 of 10* invalid strings come back `200 + success:true + code:0`, with the failure only visible in `resolutionStatus=FAILED`; `vcVerificationStatus` is `SKIPPED` in *10 of 10*; the empty string is rejected with HTTP 400. A caller that branches on `success` or `code` records a failed resolution as a success.

It does **not** show the platform verifies signatures, and it does not show that any of this is wrong behaviour -- the platform may define `success` as "the request was understood". It shows only that the word a caller is most likely to branch on does not carry the verdict. Re-run it before quoting it: a service can change, and `--run` fails loudly when the observation no longer holds.
