# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project is a technical collaboration space. We want participation to be
possible regardless of experience level, language background, nationality,
gender, disability, or employer. Contributors and maintainers pledge to make
participation harassment-free for everyone.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be precise.** If you disagree, say what specifically is wrong and what
  evidence would change your mind. "This is bad" is not a review.
- **Attack the claim, not the claimant.** Verification work invites scepticism,
  and scepticism is welcome. Contempt is not.
- **Assume competence.** Most disagreements here come from different mental
  models of the same protocol, not from bad faith.
- **Say what you don't know.** "I have not tested this" is a complete and
  respectable sentence.
- **Respect the language gap.** This project's maintainer works in Chinese and
  English; many participants work in English as a second or third language.
  Do not treat phrasing, grammar, or response latency as a proxy for rigour.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments about a person.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Deliberate misrepresentation of another contributor's position, or of a
  project's claims, in order to win an argument.
- **Presenting a scoped proof as an absolute guarantee** in project material,
  external talks, or marketing. In a project whose entire subject is
  credibility, overclaiming is a conduct issue as well as a technical one.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when an individual is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to **zhaoxinghua06@126.com**.

Reports are handled by the maintainer. Responses will be proportionate:
a private clarification, a formal warning, temporary suspension, or a permanent
ban. Because this is a single-maintainer project, we will say plainly that our
capacity for mediation is limited, and we would rather be decisive than slow.

## Attribution

This policy is written for this project. It is informed by common practice in
open-source communities, including the Contributor Covenant, but is not a
verbatim copy of any one of them.
